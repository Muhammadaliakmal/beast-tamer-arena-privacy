Beast Tamer Arena Privacy Policy

Spec Driven Games (specdrivengames.com) · Last updated 10 November 2026

Beast Tamer Arena is a creature-battling game for Android. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: we do not run any servers of our own and we never sell your information. From version 0.11.0 the game measures how it is played with Google Analytics for Firebase (see "Usage analytics" below); in the EEA, UK and Switzerland that only happens if you agree.

What stays on your device

Your progress (your creatures, their levels and records, your best depth, your daily streak and your best run's replay) is saved in the game's private storage on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If your device backs up app data to your Google account, that backup is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy. Your settings (sound, text size, language and reduced motion) are kept on your device in the same way.

Local battles

Local battle lets two people on the same Wi-Fi network play each other. When you start one, your device sends your battle name and your team directly to the other device on that network. It does not pass through the internet or reach us.

Google Play Games sign-in (optional)

You can sign in with Google Play Games. If you do, the game reads your Play Games player ID and gamer name to show that you are signed in, and uses your gamer name in local battles. The Play Games Services software that Google provides may also collect analytics and diagnostics data to keep that service working. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can manage or delete it from your Play Games profile or your Google Account. The game works fully without signing in.

Usage analytics (from version 0.11.0)

To learn which parts of the game people enjoy and where they stop playing, the game sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example that a battle started, a mission was completed, a tutorial step was reached, a creature was tamed or an item was bought, plus the game's version. Firebase also records an app instance ID (a random ID for this installation), the device model, operating system and an approximate country worked out from the IP address. We do not send your name, email, contacts or precise location, and we cannot see who you are. Collection starts switched off on every install. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it stays off unless you agree in the consent message, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy choices. The advertising ID is not collected for analytics. The data is kept for 14 months and is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy information. Uninstalling the game resets the app instance ID.

Purchases (optional)

From version 0.11.0 the shop sells gem packs, a season pass, a starter pack and a supporter bundle; gems buy cosmetic items such as skins, emblems and outfits. Every item shows its contents and price before you buy, and nothing random is sold. Earlier versions sold two one-time items, a Supporter Pack and an Aura Collection. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see card or payment details. The game only learns which items your Google account owns, keeps that on your device, and asks Google Play again to restore them on a new device. Google Play's handling of payments is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Families can require a parent's approval for purchases in Google Play's parental controls.

Rewarded ads (optional)

Some screens offer a short video ad in return for a bonus, such as a revive or extra coins. An ad only plays when you tap that button, never at launch or on its own, and you can skip the offer. At most 10 play a day. The ads are served by Google AdMob. In versions before 0.11.0 every ad request is marked as directed to children, so the ads are not personalised and the advertising ID is not used. From version 0.11.0 the game is for players aged 13 and over: ads are only personalised for verified adults, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland only if you agree in the consent message (change it under Settings > Privacy choices). AdMob may still process limited technical information (such as the device type, operating system and IP address) to deliver an ad, prevent fraud and count views, as described in Google's privacy information for AdMob and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information.

Sharing a result

When you tap Share on a results card, the game saves a picture of that card to your device's Pictures folder (Pictures/Beast Tamer) and opens Android's share menu, so you choose where it goes. The game does not read your photos and uploads nothing itself.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device by the game itself, respect quiet hours, and can be turned off in Settings. No server sends them.

What we do not do

  • No banner or pop-up ads, and no personalised ads for anyone under 18.
  • No loot boxes or random paid rewards.
  • No servers of our own, and we never sell your information.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.
  • No chat.

Links to other sites

The Discord button opens our community server in your browser or the Discord app. What happens there is covered by Discord's own privacy policy.

Children

From version 0.11.0 the game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Players under 18, and players whose age is not known, never get personalised ads. Earlier versions were made to be suitable for children and collect no analytics. Sign-in with Google Play Games is optional; for children with supervised accounts, Google requires a parent's approval before a Play Games profile can be used. Parents who have questions can contact us at the address below.

Changes

If the game starts to handle information differently, for example by adding online features, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
